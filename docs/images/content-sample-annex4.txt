MISSIONS
1) Sip like a little fish (nutrition) — Drink water 3 times today — morning, lunch, and playtime. (+4 Stars)
2) Find shady play spots (heat) — Play under a tree, porch, or indoors when it feels too hot. (+4 Stars)
3) Superhero elbow sneeze (germs) — Practice coughing or sneezing into your elbow cape. (+3 Stars)
4) Soap bubble wash (germs) — Wash hands with soap before eating — sing while you scrub! (+3 Stars)

Use: Supervised child selects Ages 5–8 in /play and completes checkable missions. Optional climate-unlocked missions from live local guidance. Progress in browser localStorage only (no child accounts).
